# The Complex2000: a plan (approved choices, step 1 being built)

A reason to collect loads of beans. Every bean goes into Fart Man's **Bean Jar**. You spend the jar aboard
**the Complex2000** (named by our chief designer, age 6): Fart Man's spaceship, which hovers above the
level you're on on the 3D world map. **Grandma** runs the shop aboard.

## Decided
- **Name**: the Complex2000. It's a spaceship, not a blimp.
- **Beans in the jar are kept forever.** The jar only goes down when you spend beans.
- **Upgrades on or off**: one setting in the main menu (UPGRADES: ON / OFF), for Dad.
- **Shopkeeper**: Grandma. She pops up at every counter. Very few words: "OOOH! 250 BEANS?"
- Beans no longer need to give gas (it refills by itself), so **Super Beans is gone**. Speedy Sneakers and
  Springy Pants take its place.

## The loop
1. Play a stage and grab beans. A counter on the HUD shows the beans grabbed in this stage.
2. At the end of the stage, "+312 BEANS!" pours into a big jar on screen, with a cha-ching and a toot.
3. On the 3D map the Complex2000 hovers over the level you're on. Press Y to go aboard. Inside, walk to
   a counter and press jump. Grandma pops up and you shop.
4. Buy something. It works straight away in every stage. Go back for more beans: replay a favourite
   stage, or raid the bean rooms (about 110 beans each, 10 rooms in the game).

## Beans and the jar
- The beans you grab go in a "this stage" pot. The pot is banked into the jar at every toilet, at the end
  of the stage, when you quit from the pause menu, and when you lose all your hearts. Banked beans are
  never lost.
- Getting hurt spills up to 6 beans from the unbanked pot. Grab them back within 5 seconds or they're gone.
  Spills no longer take gas.
- Replaying pays: beans come back every time you play a stage.
- The game stays beatable with no upgrades. They make it easier and more fun; they're never needed.
- Prices: the first buy should be affordable after 1-1, then about one buy per stage on a first
  play-through. All prices live in one table, so they're easy to change.

## Golden Beans unlock things (they're never spent)
There are 36 Golden Beans (3 per stage). Collecting them opens parts of the ship:
- **6**: the Trophy Room (a shelf of the Golden Beans and bosses you've beaten) and a free gold crown.
- **12**: the Jukebox (play any song from the game).
- **18**: **Bean Sprout**, a little pet bean that floats behind you and grabs beans near you.
- **24**: **The Bean Vault**, a secret bonus stage on the map that's nothing but bean rooms and bouncers.
- **36**: the Golden Fart Man suit and a gold Complex2000.

The ship grows as you go. New rooms light up, Grandma's kitchen gets busier, and flags go up for each
boss beaten.

## Full price list, roughly
Kitchen 2,850 + Gym 3,450 + Hot Sauce Lab 3,350 + Pantry 3,400 + Garage 2,700 + Wardrobe about 4,000 =
**about 19,500 beans**. That's about 5 play-throughs grabbing nearly everything (about 3,900 each).
Replays and bean rooms are quick, but if it feels slow we'll cut prices. If he's buying something after
nearly every stage, it's about right.

## How it would be built (the technical bit)
- **Save**: the save file gets `bean_jar`, `upgrades` (name and level), `pantry` (bought pickups),
  `wardrobe_owned` and `wardrobe_worn`. The main menu setting `upgrades_on` lives with the other options.
- **Counting**: beans tell the stage when they're eaten. The HUD shows "beans this stage".
- **The table**: one script lists every upgrade and pickup: name, room, picture, levels, prices, and what
  it changes.
- **Applying upgrades**: a small helper runs at the start of every stage (unless UPGRADES is OFF). It sets
  the player's numbers: tank size, hearts, run speed, jump height, fireball count, chilli time, air jumps
  and magnet range. The magnet, Super Stomp and Secret Sniffer are small new nodes on the player.
- **Mystery Crates**: a new kit piece placed in every stage. It picks from Heart, Chilli and Hot Sauce
  plus whatever the Pantry has sold you. Each new pickup is its own small script and scene.
- **The ship**: a hub scene made by the builder. Fart Man walks along the inside of the Complex2000, past
  the rooms. At each counter Grandma pops up with a big, simple shop panel: pictures, a bean price, and a
  jar that shows how many more beans you need. Up and down to pick, jump to buy, back to leave.
- **The map**: the 3D world map's ship becomes the Complex2000, a cartoon spaceship with the name on its
  side and Fart Man in the cockpit. The map already has a `board_ship()` hook for going aboard with Y.
- **Tests**: buying takes beans and can't go below zero, and the jar never goes down any other way.
  Upgrades and pickups are saved and come back after reloading. Each upgrade really changes the player in
  a stage, and UPGRADES OFF turns them all off. Crates only give pickups you own. Banking works at toilets,
  stage end, quitting and losing all hearts. The map gets you to the ship and back.

## Build order (each step is playable on its own)
1. **The Bean Jar** (being built now): counting, banking, the HUD counter, the end-of-stage pour, the jar
   total on the map, spills taking from the pot, and the map ship becoming the Complex2000.
2. **The ship and the shop**: Grandma and the Kitchen, Gym, Hot Sauce Lab and Garage, plus the main menu
   UPGRADES setting.
3. **The Pantry and Mystery Crates**: the crates in every stage and the six new pickups.
4. **The Wardrobe**: hats, fart colours, fart sounds, ship paint.
5. **Golden Bean unlocks**: the Trophy Room, Jukebox, Bean Sprout, the Bean Vault, the golden suit, and the
   ship growing.
