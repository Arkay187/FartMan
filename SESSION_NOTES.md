## Session 2 (2026-11-16): charge jump

### Decisions made
- The charge jump replaces the simple jump in scripts/player.gd (no separate script yet).
- Hold times: under 0.15s = Hop, 0.15s = Toot, 0.5s = Trump, 1.0s = Thunder, 1.6s = Pfft.
- Over-holding fires the pfft by itself at 1.6s, so you can see it fizzle. You must press again to recharge.
- You can only charge on the ground. Walking off a ledge cancels the charge.
- He walks at half speed while charging.
- Signals for later sounds and effects: charge_started, tier_changed(name), farted(name).
- No art yet, so feedback is: fart name printed in the Output panel, and an optional squat
  (drag the MeshInstance3D into "Squash Target" on the Player).
- Script parses cleanly in headless Godot 4.7.2. Not yet play-tested: no scenes exist.

## Session 3 (2026-11-16): scenes built

### Decisions made
- Claude built the scenes (asked for by Rich). They were generated by a one-off Godot script
  run headless, so the engine wrote the .tscn files; nothing was hand-edited.
- scenes/player.tscn: Player (CharacterBody3D + player.gd) > Visual (Node3D at his feet) >
  MeshInstance3D (capsule), plus CollisionShape3D (capsule). Squash Target = Visual, so he
  squats with his feet on the ground.
- scenes/test_level.tscn: Floor, three ledges (TootLedge, TrumpLedge, ThunderLedge) sized so
  each needs that fart or bigger, Player, Camera3D, DirectionalLight3D.
- test_level.tscn is the project's main scene, so F5 runs it.

### Tested
- Automated headless run with fake key presses: Hop 0.77 m, Toot 1.70 m, Trump 3.23 m,
  Thunder 5.25 m, Pfft 0.35 m. Walking 6 m/s, Z stays 0.
- Not yet seen by human eyes: camera framing, lighting and how it feels.

### Faster charging (same day)
- Rich wants a fast-paced game, so charge times were roughly halved:
  Toot 0.1s, Trump 0.25s, Thunder 0.5s, Pfft 0.85s (was 0.15 / 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.6).
- Launch powers unchanged.
- No slowdown while charging (removed). He runs at full speed the whole time;
  the fun is in timing the release.

### Ledges spread out (same day)
- The capsule (1 m wide) was getting stuck in the 1 m gaps between ledges.
- Ledges now have 4 m gaps (x = 2, 9, 16), the floor is 44 m wide, and the camera is
  pulled back to fit. Rule of thumb for later levels: gaps of at least 3 m, or none.
