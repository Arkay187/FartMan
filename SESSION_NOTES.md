## Session 2 (2026-11-16): charge jump

### Decisions made
- The charge jump replaces the simple jump in scripts/player.gd (no separate script yet).
- Hold times: under 0.15s = Hop, 0.15s = Toot, 0.5s = Trump, 1.0s = Thunder, 1.6s = Pfft.
- Over-holding fires the pfft by itself at 1.6s, so you can see it fizzle. You must press again to recharge.
- You can only charge on the ground. Walking off a ledge cancels the charge.
- He walks at half speed while charging.
- Signals for later sounds and effects: charge_started, tier_changed(name), farted(name).
- No art yet, so feedback is: fart name printed in the Output panel, and an optional squat
  (drag the MeshInstance3D into "Squash Target" on the Player).
- Script parses cleanly in headless Godot 4.7.2. Not yet play-tested: no scenes exist.

## Session 5 (2026-11-16): belly charge (replaces squat, Hop and Pfft)

### Decisions made (Rich's redesign)
- No more squashing capsule. Holding jump grows a belly (pink sphere proxy, Visual/Belly).
- Three jumps only: Toot = tap (small, free), Trump = hold (medium, 20 gas),
  Thunder = belly full (big, 35 gas). Hop and Pfft tiers are gone.
- Times: tap under 0.15s, belly full at 0.5s, stays full 0.35s, then leaks for 1.5s.
- Holding too long = long squeaky fart: belly deflates, gas leaks at 10 per second.
  It never loops or fails: letting go always jumps with whatever belly is left
  (Trump while some belly remains, Toot once it is empty).
- Claude's interpretation, not spelled out by Rich: releasing mid-squeak gives a Trump,
  and not enough gas drops you to the next jump down instead of failing.
- He now turns to face the way he walks, so the belly sticks out in front.
- Ledges lowered to fit the new jump heights (Toot 1.19 m, Trump 2.74 m, Thunder 5.25 m).

### Tested
- Automated headless run covered: tap, half charge, full charge, release mid-squeak,
  holding 4 seconds (one squeak, no jump until release, 15 gas leaked), empty tank, facing.
- Not checked by eye: how the belly looks and whether the timing feels right.

## Session 8 (2026-11-16): build step 1, enemies, stomping and hearts

### Decisions made
- scripts/burpling.gd + scenes/burpling.tscn: green blob (Area3D) that waddles back and forth.
  It does not use physics or gravity, so place it on the ground by hand (middle 0.4 m above floor).
- Stomp rule: he must be falling (or landed in the last 0.12s) AND be above it or right over it.
  Anything else costs a heart. Deliberately generous.
- Stomping: bounce 9 (1.5 m), or 13 (3.3 m) if jump is held on landing, and +5 gas.
- scripts/hearts.gd (Hearts node under Player): 3 hearts. scripts/hearts_bar.gd: red squares on the HUD.
- Getting hurt: lose a heart, knocked back, blink for 1.5s and can't be hurt while blinking.
- No hearts left, or falling off the level: back to the start with full hearts, blinking briefly.
- Squash animation scales the blob model, not the Area3D (Jolt physics rejects squashed areas).
- Test level has 4 Burplings on the floor.

### Tested
- Automated headless run: side touch loses a heart and knocks back, no second hit while blinking,
  dropping on one squashes it and bounces (1.55 m, +5 gas), holding jump bounces higher (3.27 m),
  last heart respawns at the start with 3 hearts. Jump, gas, camera and fall tests still pass.
- Not checked by eye: the blink, the squash, the hearts bar, and whether stomping feels fair.

## Session 12 (2026-11-16): build step 5, world map, saving, storybook and opening story

### Decisions made
- scripts/save_game.gd (`class_name SaveData`, autoload named `SaveGame`, registered by the
  scene builder through ProjectSettings so the engine wrote project.godot). Saves JSON to
  `user://save.json`: per stage id cleared / best Golden Beans / best score, "seen" flags and
  the last map stop. Other scripts use `SaveLookup.find(self)`, which returns null if the
  autoload is missing, and then just carry on without saving.
- Tests never touch the real save: a headless run saves to `user://save_headless.json`, and
  tests switch to their own file with `use_file(path)`. (In a `--script` test the autoload IS
  present, but its `_ready` runs late, so `use_file` marks the path as chosen and `_ready`
  leaves it alone.)
- Stage (scripts/stage.gd) has `stage_id` (test_level is "1-1"), `return_delay` (4 s),
  `skip_after` (0.8 s) and `map_scene`. When the can opens it records the result, keeps the
  banner up, then goes to the world map after the delay or when jump is pressed. F6 on
  test_level still works.
- World map (scenes/world_map.tscn): Control. Stops, worlds and positions are plain lists in
  scripts/map_stops.gd. scripts/world_map.gd does the walking rules, scripts/map_canvas.gd
  draws it (a colour band per world, dotted path, round spots, bigger red bosses, light-blue
  flight stops, flag and three bean dots on cleared stops), scripts/map_marker.gd is Fart Man.
  Left/right = one stop with a 0.25 s slide, never past the first uncleared stop. Jump enters;
  only 1-1 has a scene, the rest show COMING SOON!. The marker starts on the last stop
  entered/cleared. The stop name is big at the top, "GOLDEN BEANS n/3  BEST score" at the bottom.
- Storybook (scenes/storybook.tscn, scripts/storybook.gd): coloured card (or the picture, if
  the page's image file exists) with one big line under it. Gentle wobble, slow zoom and a pop
  on each page. Jump turns the page; after the last page it emits `finished` and changes to
  `next_scene` (the map). Hold jump for 1 s to skip everything.
- Pages are in scripts/story_pages.gd (the 5-page opening). Pages point at
  res://art/story/opening_1.png ... opening_5.png; drop drawings there and they appear.
- Boot (scenes/boot.tscn, scripts/boot.gd) is now the main scene: shows the opening story
  the first time (flag "intro_story", marked as seen when it starts), else the world map.
- Claude's interpretations: "seen" is set when the story starts, not when it ends, so
  skipping or quitting never shows it twice. A stage without an id (empty) saves nothing.
