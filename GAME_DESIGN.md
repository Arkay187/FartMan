# Fart Man: Game Design (approved 2026-11-16)

A fast, silly 2.5D platformer for a 6 year old and his dad. One button does almost everything.

## The very silly story

Grandma grows the world's most powerful beans: **Thunder Beans**. One bowl and you can fart
yourself over a house.

**Big Bad Burp** hates this. He is a huge, fizzy, grumpy blob who believes air should only
ever come out of the TOP end. One night he steals every bean in the world, corks up the
Bean Factory, and flies off to his Fizzy Fortress on **Planet Whiff**, an alien planet made
entirely of smelly gas. His plan: a world where
everybody burps and nobody farts. Ever.

He missed one bowl. A boy ate it for breakfast. He is now **Fart Man**, and he is going to
get the beans back, one toot at a time.

**The ending:** it turns out Big Bad Burp has never farted in his life. He is just full of
trapped wind and very, very uncomfortable. Fart Man beats him, feeds him a bean, and Big Bad
Burp does the biggest fart in history. It blows the cork out of the factory, rains beans on
the whole world, and he feels MUCH better. Everybody farts. The end.

## Controls

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Left / Right | Run |
| Jump button (Space / pad A) | Toot: instant small hop, free, from the ground |
| Fart button (F or Z / pad X or B), hold, let go | Trump: medium jump. The belly grows while you hold. Works in the air too: a timed double jump |
| Fart (let go when belly is full) | Thunder: big jump |
| Fart (hold too long) | Long squeaky leak. Belly deflates, gas is wasted |
| Either button (flight stages) | Hold to fart upwards, let go to drop |

That is the whole control scheme: run, jump, fart. A fart tap that is too short, or with no gas, just fizzles.

## Core rules

- **Gas tank.** Trump and Thunder cost gas. Toots are free, so you can never get stuck.
- **Gas comes back by itself.** The tank slowly refills all the time (empty to full in about
  12 seconds), after a short pause each time gas is used. It does not refill while he is
  holding a charge in. So running out is never the end: wait a moment, or go and find beans.
- **Beans are the fast way.** A bean gives a big chunk of gas straight away, and points.
- **Score.** Beans 10, squashing an enemy 50, Golden Bean 100, opening the Bean Can 500.
  The score shows on screen and on the STAGE CLEAR banner. Best scores will show on the map.
- **Stomping.** Land on top of an enemy and it is squashed with a "pop". You bounce off
  it for free, and get a little gas back. Hold jump as you land to bounce higher.
- **Hearts.** Three hearts. Touching an enemy from the side costs one heart and you flash
  for a moment. No lives and no Game Over: lose all hearts and you pop back to the last toilet.
- **Toilets are checkpoints.** Run past a toilet, it flushes, and that is where you come back.
- **Golden Beans.** Three hidden in every stage. Optional. They show as stars on the map.
- **The goal.** Every stage ends at a giant locked **Bean Can**. Thunder-jump onto the lid
  to pop it open and free the beans.

## Power-ups

| Power-up | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **Chilli Pepper** | Hot farts! Run and jump much faster, flame trail, run straight through enemies | About 6 seconds |
| **Mega Bean Can** | Gas tank completely full, right now | Instant |
| **Rocket Burrito** | FART-POWERED FLIGHT. Starts a flight sequence | Until the sequence ends |

## Flight sequences (the crazy bits)

The Rocket Burrito turns the game into a side-scrolling flying stage.

- The screen scrolls by itself, fast. Fart Man flies with a huge rainbow gas trail.
- Hold jump to rise, let go to sink. Gas never runs out while flying.
- Collect lines of beans, fly through hoops, dodge things, and squash flying enemies by
  bumping them from above.
- The camera pulls right back to show off the whole landscape below.
- Hitting something costs a heart and a bounce, but never stops the flight.

Each world has one full flight stage, and short surprise flights can be hidden in normal stages.

## Enemies

| Enemy | Where | What it does | How to beat it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Burpling** | Garden, Factory | Small green blob, waddles back and forth | Stomp |
| **Hiccup** | Garden onwards | Hops on the spot, jumping high every few seconds | Stomp when it lands |
| **Grumpy Gnome** | Garden | Stands still, throws a mud pie now and then | Stomp |
| **Prickle** | Garden onwards | Spiky. Hurts if you land on it | Jump over it, or use a Chilli |
| **Cork Bot** | Factory | Rolls quickly along platforms | Stomp |
| **Fizz Bat** | Factory | Flies in a wave | Stomp it, or use it as a stepping stone |
| **Burp Bubble** | Planet Whiff | Floats upwards slowly | Stomp it to bounce very high |
| **Whiffler** | Planet Whiff | Little alien that puffs out a stink cloud you must not touch | Stomp it between puffs |
| **Jellyfish** | Planet Whiff | Glowing alien jelly that bobs and drifts in the air | Stomp it from above |

No old enemies in space: Planet Whiff only has its own aliens (Whifflers and jellyfish), even in
the Fizzy Fortress and the boss fight. (His idea: gnomes and cork bots don't live in space.)

## The world map

An old-school saga map: a winding dotted path across a picture of the world, with a round
spot for each stage.

- Fart Man stands on the map. Left and right walk him along the path, jump enters a stage.
- Cleared stages show a little flag and their Golden Bean stars.
- You can only walk as far as the first stage you have not cleared yet.
- Beating a boss opens the path to the next world (a silly short cut-scene, then a fart-flight
  across the map to the new world).
- You can go back and replay any cleared stage.
- Progress saves automatically.

## Worlds and stages

Three worlds. Each is two running stages, one flight stage and a boss: 12 stops on the map.

### World 1: Grandma's Garden
Teaches everything. Bright, gentle, hard to fail.

1. **The Veg Patch.** Learn Toot, Trump and Thunder. Burplings. First beans.
2. **The Greenhouse.** Hiccups, Gnomes and Prickles. First Chilli Pepper: a downhill dash.
3. **Over the Rooftops (flight).** First Rocket Burrito. Fly over the garden, the village,
   washing lines and a very surprised cow.
4. **Boss: Sneezy Weezy.**

### World 2: The Bean Factory
Conveyor belts, pipes and steam. Faster and trickier.

1. **The Conveyor Belts.** Moving floors, Cork Bots, Mega Bean Cans.
2. **The Pipe Maze.** Steam vents that boost your jumps, Fizz Bats as stepping stones.
3. **Up the Chimney (flight).** Shoot up and out of the giant chimney, then across the
   factory roofs through clouds of bean steam.
4. **Boss: Colonel Cork.**

### World 3: Planet Whiff
An alien gas planet. No solid ground: everything is swirling coloured clouds, floating
rocks and alien plants. Jumping works exactly the same as everywhere else (normal gravity).

1. **The Cloud Tops.** Squishy gas clouds you sink into and bounce off, Burp Bubbles to
   bounce on, and **gas geysers** that fill your tank and blast you upwards. Whifflers.
2. **The Fizzy Fortress.** Everything from the whole game, mixed together.
3. **The Stink Storm (flight).** Fly through a giant swirling gas storm: lightning,
   rainbow clouds, floating alien jellyfish and the planet's rings.
4. **Final Boss: Big Bad Burp.**

## Bosses

All bosses work the same simple way: dodge the attack, wait for the dizzy moment, stomp.
Three stomps wins. A toilet checkpoint sits right outside every boss door.

- **Sneezy Weezy** (Garden). A giant flower with terrible hay fever. Sneezes clouds of pollen
  along the ground: jump over them. After three sneezes it droops, worn out. Stomp its head.
  It gets faster each time.
- **Colonel Cork** (Factory). A huge cork robot who plugged up the factory. Charges across
  the room: Thunder-jump over him. He bonks into the wall and sees stars. Stomp. After each
  hit he also shoots corks out of his hat.
- **Big Bad Burp** (Planet Whiff). Two parts. First: he burps giant bubbles. Bounce up the
  bubbles like stairs to reach him and stomp his head. Second: he flies off in a huff, you
  grab a Rocket Burrito, and the last fight is a flying chase around the planet's rings.

## Telling the story

The story is told three ways, from cheapest to fanciest. All of them use very few words,
because the main player is six.

### 1. Speech bubbles (used everywhere)
- A bubble pops up over a character's head, pointing at whoever is talking.
- Big letters, one short line at a time, about six words at most. "GIVE ME BACK MY BEANS!"
- Each character gets a silly noise instead of a voice: Fart Man toots, Big Bad Burp burps,
  Grandma goes "ooooh".
- Press jump for the next bubble. Bubbles during play (hints, enemy grumbles) fade by
  themselves and never stop the game.
- One reusable bubble that any script can call: say who is talking and what they say.

### 2. Puppet-show scenes (short, made inside the game)
Little acted-out scenes using the normal game characters on the normal game stage.
No special art needed, so they work even with grey boxes.

- A scene is a simple list of steps, read top to bottom:
  walk here, say this, wait, jump, shake the screen, do a fart, look surprised.
- The list lives in one small file per scene, written so a parent and child can read it
  and change the words or add their own jokes.
- The camera can zoom in on a face or pan to show something.
- Rules: under 15 seconds, always skippable by holding jump, and only shown the first time
  (not again every time you retry a boss).

Where they go:
- **Boss hello:** the boss shows off and says something rude. Two or three bubbles.
- **Boss goodbye:** the boss is beaten in a silly way (Sneezy Weezy sneezes its own petals off).
- **World arrival:** one quick gag when you first land in a new world.
- **Stage end:** the Bean Can pops, beans fly everywhere, Fart Man does a victory toot.

### 3. Storybook pages (the big moments)
Full-screen pictures with a line of text underneath, like turning the pages of a picture book.
Press jump to turn the page. Each page can wobble, slide or zoom a little so it feels alive.

- **The beginning:** about five pages. Grandma's beans, Big Bad Burp steals them,
  the boy eats the last bowl, FART MAN!
- **Between worlds:** one or two pages, then the fart-flight across the map.
- **The ending:** about five pages, finishing on the biggest fart in history.
- **The pictures can be our own drawings.** Draw them on paper, photograph them, drop them
  in. Placeholder pages are plain coloured cards with the text on.
- **Optional:** record our own voices reading each page.

### Bringing characters to life
- With grey boxes: squash, stretch, bounce, wobble and shake, all done in code. Enough to be funny.
- With real art later: proper animations per character (idle, walk, jump, charge, hurt, talk),
  made in Godot's animation tools and played by the same scene lists.
- Faces matter most. Even the grey-box Fart Man gets two eyes that can look surprised.

### What this needs us to build
| Piece | What it is | When |
|---|---|---|
| Speech bubble | One reusable bubble, callable from any script | With step 2 |
| Scene player | Runs a list of steps (walk, say, wait, shake...) | Before the first boss |
| Storybook viewer | Shows pages with text, jump to turn | With the world map |
| "Seen it" memory | Saved with progress so scenes do not repeat | With the world map |

## Build order

Each step is playable on its own, so we can stop and play after every one.

1. Enemies, stomping and hearts (Burpling first).
2. Toilet checkpoints, Golden Beans, the Bean Can goal, and speech bubbles.
3. Power-ups: Mega Bean Can, then Chilli Pepper.
4. Flight mode and the Rocket Burrito.
5. World map with saving, and the storybook viewer with the opening story.
6. (DONE, needs playing) Scene player, then World 1 complete: three stages and Sneezy Weezy with its hello and
   goodbye scenes. **This is the first "real game".**
7. World 2, then World 3 and the ending.
8. Sounds, our own storybook drawings and proper art, replacing the grey boxes.
   The plan for character models and animations is in ART_PLAN.md (draft). Its first phase,
   the animation hooks that work with grey boxes, can be built at any time.

## Changes from the original plan

- One boss has become three (one per world). Big Bad Burp is still the final boss.
- Garden, Bean Factory and Space are now worlds with four stops each, not single levels.
- Space is Planet Whiff, an alien gas planet. No low gravity anywhere in the game.
